HOW IT WORKS

Three steps, and only one of them is yours.

01

Forward a receipt

Any subscription confirmation or invoice, from any vendor, to your private Bleed address. Or set a mail filter and never think about it again.

02

Bleed reads it

Amount, currency, monthly or yearly, next renewal date. Anything it can't read confidently goes to a review queue with the email attached — never silently dropped.

03

You get warned

Three days before each charge, by email and on your phone, at 9am in your own timezone. Then you decide: keep it, or go and cancel it.

FAQ

Questions.

Do I have to forward every receipt by hand?

Only the first one. After that, set a rule in Gmail or Outlook that auto-forwards anything from your subscription vendors, and the ledger maintains itself.

What if it reads a receipt wrongly?

Anything it isn't confident about goes to a review queue with the original email attached, so you can see what it saw and correct it in one step. Nothing is dropped silently.

Does it cancel subscriptions for me?

No. Bleed tells you what's about to charge you and what you've stopped using; cancelling stays with you and the vendor. You can mark something cancelled here to keep the record straight.

Which currencies are supported?

Naira, cedi, shilling and rand alongside dollars, pounds, euros, Canadian and Australian dollars, and yen. Each subscription keeps the currency it actually bills in.

Can I use it without notifications?

Yes. Email reminders are on by default and push is opt-in; you can switch either off per subscription, as long as one channel stays on.
